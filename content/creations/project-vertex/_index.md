**[Project Vertex]** is a four-part episodic coming-of-age game shown through the imaginative eyes of a ten-year-old in a fictional suburb inspired by France's Bretagne region, during the 1990s.

---

## About This Game

Alexandre Lyon is ten years old, and their world, just like the town, is getting smaller by the season. Fewer kids are in the neighborhood, weird things are happening in the apartment, fears turn into living enemies, and strange neighbors are moving in next door. The one consistent thing in their life is Grandpa's fantastical stories — stories that blend reality and help them rediscover imagination that will set them on a path of understanding and acceptance.

Unlock new areas to explore each episode, investigate hidden truths behind grandiose stories, face your fears turned enemies, make life-changing choices, and remember…

---

## Features

- Keyboard, Xbox, and PlayStation Controller Supported
- Explore a vast, hand-drawn, non-AI-generated, rich-in-detail world that blurs reality and imagination

---

## Game Mechanics

**Dual Perception** — Use your newfound special ability to change the perspectives of an area. What was once a vibrant forest can quickly become a medieval battleground!

**Memory Input System** — Make difficult decisions and respond using an integrated dialogue response. When you select a choice, know that there will be consequences later down the road… Or make silly choices — the point is it's up to you to decide!

## Trotson Radio

- Over 3 hours of original music
- A soundtrack that features indie songs and score tracks
- Including in-studio created 9D audio — an immersive, headphone-based listening experience that simulates sound moving in a 3D, 360-degree space around the player's head
- Discover new indie artists featured on our in-game radio **PêcheFM**
- In a game where we celebrate community, we also aim to support new artists
