Rustling Leaves Games is looking for a **Graphic Designer** to help bring our game and studio brand to life visually.

We're looking for someone who can create:
- Game title/logo design
- Help design and organize a publisher pitch deck art
- Steam store graphics and front-page assets
- Social media and promotional graphics
- Marketing materials and campaign assets
- Additional visual assets as needed throughout development

You'll work closely with our development and creative teams to make sure our visual identity feels cohesive, memorable, and true to the heart of our games.

**This is a Rev-Share role, but we strive to create a professional, supportive, and
growth-focused environment.**

---

## Ideal Candidate
Someone with strong graphic design skills, a good eye for visual storytelling, and experience creating branding or marketing materials for games or creative projects. If you love games, storytelling, and making things look really good, we'd love to hear from you!

---

## Bonus Points
- Experience with creature or non-human design
- Familiarity with 3D pipelines and working alongside 3D artists
- Background in game development or environmental storytelling
- Experience creating promotional or key art
